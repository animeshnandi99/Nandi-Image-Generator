---
name: NandiAi brand and launch rules
description: Visual restrictions and publication requirements for the NandiAi website.
---

Avoid a generic AI-product look. Do not use purple gradients, pill-shaped buttons, fake reviews or metrics, fake customer counters, vague hero text, emoji icons, em dashes, excessive scroll animation, AI-generated-looking photos or copy, or cursor animation.

Do not launch or publish until a custom domain is connected, a favicon is added, any "Made with AI" tag is removed, and Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions pages are present.

**Why:** the user asked.

**How to apply:** follow these visual restrictions in NandiAi pages and treat every listed launch item as a blocker before suggesting publication.
